## Storyboard mapping

- Scene 1 / Skill Issue: S08 for diagonal impact and action readability.
- Scene 2 / Crossing: S04 for a frame inside the environment.
- Scene 3 / First room visit: S06 for quiet low light; D01 for speaker-centred dialogue.
- Scene 4 / Still EZ: S03 for horizontal staging.
- Scene 5 / Work voice: S05 for a lit screen; D02 for a close exchange.
- Scene 6 / Final reply: D03 for holding dialogue in a wide shot.

Study composition, text placement and contrast. Draw original Lapu-Lapu rooms and characters. Rights for model training are not established by a publicly accessible screenshot. Use owned or permission-cleared art for a Krea training pack.
