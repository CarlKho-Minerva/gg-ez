### Page text of 2026-10-31

GG EZ is a five-minute game about the first rung: the reflex to type something cruel at a stranger who beat you. The match is rigged so you lose by a hair, she types "ez", and you get three seconds and two replies. Lose three times and RAGE QUIT appears. Press it and the controller goes through the screen; on the other side is her room in Quezon City at 2 AM, her night-shift lanyard, and her phone. Then she queues again and you get the same two buttons. The game logs what you pick, and how fast, every time.

Controls: A/D or arrows to move, W to jump, J to swing. Space advances. Mouse for the room.

Made for the Checkpoint Game Jam, theme Play to Prevent, by Carl Kho and Dain Kim. Source: https://github.com/CarlKho-Minerva/gg-ez. Concept brief and AI-use disclosure are attached.

### What is playable now, and the roadmap

This submission is the playable first act: the rigged near-miss match, the three-second "ez" reply window, rage quit, the first room visits, and the queue-again loop with the same two buttons.

After the jam, the remaining four acts are built from storyboards that are already drawn as editable Figma grayboxes: the second match; her night shift and the customer call, the scene we protect; her family and her friend; the final fair fight and the same two buttons. Each scene has its exact dialogue, camera and timing written down. In that version the story moves to Lapu-Lapu City, with Cebuano at home and English on screen; the build you are playing still says Quezon City.

Carl, talking it through on the day of submission: that story tree "will be added to our credits and roadmap. Just so I can tell the judges that this is actually the story we want to be in the next two months."

The design document and the recovered storyboard, scene by scene, are in docs/GDD.md in the repository.
