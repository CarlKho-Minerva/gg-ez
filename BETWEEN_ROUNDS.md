## Decisions to preserve

The player sees and controls a haunted-house-style ghost, invisible and intangible to Lia. Twine is a temporary narrative design and testing tool. Godot is the final game engine. Keep the repeated room visits, character animation, cinematic camera movement, dialogue and the final reply's repeated composition.

The live original supersedes the shorter six-panel adaptation prepared from the incomplete cached preview. The earlier adaptation is retained at [docs/recovered/earlier-short-adaptation.md](docs/recovered/earlier-short-adaptation.md) as history, not the current master.
